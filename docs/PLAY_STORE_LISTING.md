**Category:** Medical

## Full description

Help your family stay on top of everyday medicines with a calm, easy-to-read daily plan.

MediKin keeps schedules, dose status, and medicine stock together on one device. Add one or more times using quick morning, afternoon, and evening choices or select any custom time. Mark a dose taken or skipped with one tap. If a dose is still unrecorded after five minutes, the app raises a family check-in alert.

Key features:

- Daily medicine timeline with clear Due, Taken, Skipped, and Missed states
- Flexible custom reminder times
- Audible dose and five-minute missed-dose alerts
- Refill warnings based on remaining stock
- Family contact card with one-tap dialer and pre-filled message handoff
- Private local storage with no account, ads, analytics, or data upload
- Light and dark themes with large, readable controls

MediKin is an organizational aid and not a medical device. Always follow instructions from a qualified healthcare professional.

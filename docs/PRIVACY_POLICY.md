# MediKin Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

MediKin is a local-first medication reminder application. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

## Data stored on the device

The app stores medicine names, dosages, schedules, stock counts, dose history, a parent's display name, and an optional family contact name and phone number. This information remains in the app's private on-device storage and is removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

## Permissions and external actions

The notification permission is used only to show medicine and missed-dose reminders. The app does not request contacts, phone-call, or SMS permissions. If a user taps Call or Message, MediKin opens the device dialer or messaging app with the chosen number and, for messages, a suggested draft. The user reviews and sends it from that external app.

## Network and analytics

This version has no network API, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or account system. Cleartext network traffic is disabled.

## Medical notice

MediKin is an organizational aid, not a medical device or substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Users should follow the prescription and instructions provided by their clinician or pharmacist.
